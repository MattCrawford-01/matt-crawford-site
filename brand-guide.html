Brand Guide

Matt Crawford

A quick reference for the palette, type, and voice behind the Matt Crawford site — monochrome, image-led, and restrained. Type system: Inter throughout, for a clean, neutral, generic-by-design feel across headings and body.

01 — Color

Near-black, off-white, nothing else

Ink

#0A0A0APrimary background. Used everywhere — there is no secondary background.

Paper

#F4F3EFPrimary text and hero headlines.

Grey

#8C8C86Secondary text, captions, tags, body copy.

Ink 2

#141414Card and tile backgrounds only — never large fields.

NO ACCENT COLOR. Contrast and hierarchy come from scale, weight, and tracking — not hue. If a project ever needs a splash of color, treat it as a rare exception, not a system.

03 — Voice

Plainspoken, never breathless

Do

"Every project starts with a flight plan."

Short, declarative lines. Let the image carry the mood.

Name the client, category, and location — specifics build trust.

Avoid

"Elevate your vision to soaring new heights!"

Long paragraphs of scene-setting copy.

Stacked adjectives — "breathtaking, stunning, unforgettable."

04 — Components

Text links, with one exception

Actions are bracketed, tracked-out text links with a hairline underline — quiet, in keeping with the reference sites' restraint. The one deliberate exception is the persistent "Get in touch" button that follows the visitor down the page: it's solid-filled so it reads as the one standing invitation to make contact, and it auto-hides once the visitor reaches the Contact section itself.

05 — Video & Imagery

Full color, full video, on request

Every tile plays a short, muted, looping preview at all times — in natural color, never desaturated. Clicking any tile opens the complete, full-length clip with sound and playback controls, captioned below with Project, Client, and Credits. The hero uses the same pattern at full scale: a single muted, looping background video instead of a static image. Photography follows the same rule — natural color, no filters.
